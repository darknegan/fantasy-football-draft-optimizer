Why ESPN is not listed

ESPN has no official API. Connecting a private ESPN league would mean copying your account session cookies out of your browser and sending them to undocumented endpoints, so we left it out rather than put your account or their terms of service at risk. Set ESPN leagues up manually instead — you lose auto-sync and nothing else.
